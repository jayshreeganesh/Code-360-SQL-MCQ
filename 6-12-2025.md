## 3. Query (LIKE with patterns)

**Table**: `Employee(id, FirstName, LastName)`  

Display all employees whose `FirstName` starts with the letter `R` and ends with the letter `I`.  

**Given queries**  
a. `SELECT * FROM Employee WHERE FirstName LIKE '%RI%';`  
b. `SELECT * FROM Employee WHERE FirstName LIKE 'R%' AND FirstName LIKE '%I';`  
c. `SELECT * FROM Employee WHERE FirstName LIKE 'R' AND 'I';`  
d. `SELECT * FROM Employee WHERE FirstName LIKE 'R%I';`  

**Options (single correct)**  
- Only (c)  
- Only (b)  
- **(b) and (d)** ✅  
- (b), (c) and (d)  
- (a) and (c)  
- None of the above  

**Solution description**  
The percent sign `%` in LIKE represents any sequence of characters.  
- `LIKE 'R%'` matches values starting with `R`.  
- `LIKE '%I'` matches values ending with `I`.  
- `LIKE 'R%I'` matches values starting with `R` and ending with `I` with anything in between.
