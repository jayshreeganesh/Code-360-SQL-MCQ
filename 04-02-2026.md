## **Quiz Summary: DBMS & SQL Fundamentals**

### **1. SQL UNION Operator**

* **Problem Statement:** What does `UNION` do in SQL?
* **Correct Option:** **Combines results of two queries**
* **Solution Description:** `UNION` combines the results of two or more `SELECT` queries into a single result set.

### **2. Procedure Lookup**

* **Problem Statement:** Suppose you need to look out for the procedure containing the word "blog" (it can be anywhere in the name: at the beginning, in the middle, or end of the name). Which command should be used in SQL?
* **Correct Option:** `SHOW PROCEDURE STATUS LIKE '%blog%'`
* **Key Concept:** The `%` wildcard matches any number of characters, so placing it on both sides of "blog" ensures it finds the word anywhere in the string.

### **3. Storage Issue**

* **Problem Statement:** What is the same data being saved in multiple locations called?
* **Correct Option:** **Redundancy**
* **Solution Description:** Repeating the same data at different locations is called data redundancy. In file-based systems, it is very difficult to keep track of and manage redundancy.

### **4. Cardinality**

* **Problem Statement:** Which of the following indicates the maximum number of entities that can be involved in a relationship in DBMS?
* **Correct Option:** **Maximum cardinality**
* **Solution Description:** The maximum number of entity instances that can participate in a relationship is indicated by Maximum cardinality.

### **5. Entity Relationships**

* **Problem Statement:** In DBMS, an entity can be:
* **Correct Option:** **Related to many entities**
* **Solution Description:** An entity can be related to many different entities, including itself. For example, in a University ER diagram, the entity **Course** can be related to **Students**, **Professors**, **Fees**, **Workshops**, etc.
