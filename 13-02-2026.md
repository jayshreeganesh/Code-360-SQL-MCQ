## 5. Foreign Key 1

* **Problem Statement:** Identify the Foreign Key from table "Sales" in Database.
* **Table Client:** ClientID (primary Key), clientname, clientphone
* **Table Sales:** SalesID (Primary Key), ClientID, Profit


* **Selected Answer:** **ClientID**
* **Logic:** Since `ClientID` is the primary key in the `Client` table and is included in the `Sales` table to link the two, it acts as the Foreign Key in `Sales`.
