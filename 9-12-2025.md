## 1. Data Anomaly

**Question**
Consider a table where an employee can be in only one department but can join multiple clubs. If Gaurav moves to the Tech team and the department is updated in only one of his rows, what anomaly can occur?

**Answer**

- **Updation Anomaly**

**Reason**

- Same employee appears in multiple rows; updating only some rows causes inconsistent department values.

***

## 2. SQL DISTINCT Keyword

**Question**
What does `DISTINCT` do in SQL?

**Answer**

- **Filters duplicate rows**

**Reason**

- `SELECT DISTINCT ...` returns only unique rows in the result set.

***

## 3. Views

**Question**
SQL views are also known as:

**Answer**

- **Virtual tables**

**Reason**

- A view behaves like a table (rows and columns) but is stored as a query, not as a physical table.

***

## 4. Unary Operation

**Question**
Which is a unary operation in databases?

**Answer**

- **Generalized selection**

**Reason**

- A unary operation works on one relation; generalized selection takes a single relation as its argument.

***

## 5. Intersect Operator

**Question**
What is true regarding the `INTERSECT` operator?

**Answer**

- **INTERSECT operation returns the common rows from the combined queries.**

**Reason**

- `INTERSECT` yields only rows that appear in the results of both queries, after duplicate elimination.
