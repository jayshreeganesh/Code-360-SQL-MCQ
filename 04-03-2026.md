## 2. Multi-level Indexing

**Problem Statement:** Which of the following will be considered as multi-level indexing in Database?

* **Options:**
* Clustering indexing
* **Secondary indexing** (Correct)
* Primary Indexing
* All of them


* **Solution Description:** Secondary index is taken into consideration when the size of datafile increases i.e. when the size of the table increases, sparse indexing starts to slow down. To overcome this we introduce another level of indexing that is known as secondary indexing... Secondary indexing is also known as multilevel indexing.
