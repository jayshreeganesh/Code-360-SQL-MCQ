## **2. Disaster Recovery**
* **Problem Statement:** Most NoSQL databases support automatic __________, meaning that you get high availability and disaster recovery.
* **Selected Option:** replication
* **Solution Description:** NoSQL databases are highly available due to their auto replication feature i.e. whenever any kind of failure happens data replicates itself to the preceding consistent state. This helps in high availability and data recovery.

## **3. Transaction Permanency**
* **Problem Statement:** Which ACID property ensures that once a transaction is committed, its changes are permanent, even in the event of a system crash?
* **Selected Option:** Durability
* **Solution Description:** The Durability property guarantees that the results of a committed transaction are permanently saved to the database, ensuring data remains intact even after a system crash.

## **5. Referential Integrity**
* **Problem Statement:** Referential integrity constraint in DBMS states that foreign key should have a matching primary key or it must be not Null.
* **Selected Option:** False
* **Solution Description:** False. Referential integrity in a DBMS ensures a foreign key either matches a primary key in the referenced table or is NULL (if permitted). The statement wrongly states the foreign key "must be not NULL".
