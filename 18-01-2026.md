## 2. Club Removal (Anomalies)

**Problem Statement:** Consider the following table:

| Employee_ID | Name | Department | Clubs |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Navdeep | Content | Fitness Freaks |
| 2 | Shubhangi | Marketing | Marketing Club |
| 3 | Shubhangi | Marketing | Management Club |
| 4 | Gaurav | CIS | Technology Org. |
| 5 | Gaurav | CIS | Fitness Freaks |

Answer the following question with the help of above data. Keep in mind that an employee may be added to various clubs, but only one department. If the club "Fitness Freaks" is disbanded and the data is not handled properly, what anomaly might occur?

* **Options:**
* Updation Anomaly
* **Deletion Anomaly** (Correct)
* Insertion Anomaly
* None of the above


* **Solution Description:** Deletion of instances of the club Fitness Freak will lead to the complete loss of employee information having Employee_ID as 1.
