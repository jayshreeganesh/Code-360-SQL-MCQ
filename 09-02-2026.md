* **Question:** Which of the following Concurrency controls is prone to deadlocks?
* **Correct Answer:** Lock Based Control
* **Solution Description:** Deadlock: a situation where the transactions try to access locks on already locked data items. Whereas in timestamp, each transaction is allocated with the time slot. Hence it can't enter a deadlock.
