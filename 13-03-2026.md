## 1. SQL WHERE Clause

**Problem Statement:**
What is the purpose of the `WHERE` clause in SQL?

**Options:**

* Order data
* **Filter data** (Correct)
* Join tables
* Group data

**Solution Description:**
`WHERE` is used to filter records in a query.

## 2. ER Basics 3

**Problem Statement:**
In DBMS, the E-R modeling technique is a:

**Options:**

* **Top-down approach** (Correct)
* Bottom-up approach
* Left-right approach
* None of the above

**Solution Description:**
The E-R modeling technique is a top-down approach.

## 3. Higher Abstraction

**Problem Statement:**
In DBMS, which of the following is a type of abstraction in which entities with relationships come together to form higher level entity?

**Options:**

* Generalization
* Specialization
* **Aggregation** (Correct)
* None of the above

**Solution Description:**
Aggregation is used when we need to express a relationship among relationships. It is like abstraction through which relationships are treated as higher-level entities. In this, multiple entities are considered as a single entity and again this single entity has a relationship with another entity.

## 4. Entity Subgrouping

**Problem Statement:**
The process of designating sub groupings within the entity set is called as ___________.

**Options:**

* **Specialization** (Correct)
* Division
* Aggregation
* Finalization

**Solution Description:**
With respect to ER Model, specialisation is the procedure to split up the entities into further sub entities on the basis of their functionalities, specialties and features. These sub-designations of entities are distinctive from other entities in the set.

## 5. One to Many

**Problem Statement:**
In a one-to-many relationship, the entity that is on the one side of the relationship is called a ___________ entity in DBMS.

**Options:**

* **parent** (Correct)
* child
* instance
* subtype

**Solution Description:**
In a one-to-many relationship, the entity that is on the one side of the relationship is called a parent entity in DBMS.
